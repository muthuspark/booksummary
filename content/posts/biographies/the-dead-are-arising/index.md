## Reconstructing Malcolm X's life

*The Dead Are Arising: The Life of Malcolm X* is a biography by Les Payne and Tamara Payne. Les Payne began researching the book in 1990; Tamara Payne, his daughter and principal researcher, completed it after his death in 2018.

The book draws on interviews with people who knew Malcolm at different stages of his life. Family members, childhood acquaintances, people from his years on the streets and in prison, and figures connected with his public career contribute to the reconstruction. This method lets the authors compare the remembered public figure with accounts from his immediate surroundings.

## A changing political identity

The narrative follows the development of Malcolm's beliefs, his involvement with the Nation of Islam, and the conflicts surrounding his later independence and assassination. The emphasis is on tracing change rather than treating his public identity as fixed from the beginning.

The biography places Malcolm within the struggle for Black freedom and examines the forces that shaped both his life and its violent ending. Its research offers a fuller context for understanding his political development and the continuing arguments about his legacy.
