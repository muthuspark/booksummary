## Bibliographic note

The title *Red: The Life and Times of an American Progressive*, attributed here to D. D. Guttenplan, needs a confirmed edition or ISBN before a reliable summary can be provided. The author's published bibliography does not establish this title-and-author pairing.

Guttenplan's verified biography *American Radical: The Life and Times of I. F. Stone* concerns the independent journalist I. F. Stone. It is a separate title and should not be treated as evidence that *Red* is a biography of Stone or of William Z. Foster.

Reference: [Guttenplan's official page for *American Radical*](https://www.ddguttenplan.com/americanradical.html).
