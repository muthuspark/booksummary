# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers browsing a public library of book summaries on desktop and mobile, seeking key ideas and practical takeaways without reading a full book.

## Product Purpose

Make book summaries easy to discover, scan and read comfortably in longer sessions.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, thoughtful. Use Crimson Text throughout the site, including headings, body text and navigation. Preserve the light theme.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative magazine styling, ornamental typography and visual treatments that distract from the summaries.

## Design Principles

- Give the reading experience priority.
- Make the hierarchy clear through consistent type and spacing.
- Keep navigation and metadata subordinate to content.
- Preserve the established visual identity across articles and listings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable mobile text, comfortable line lengths, visible focus states and support for 200% zoom. Respect reduced motion preferences.
